What is gounscramble?

gounscramble is a free home for word tools, puzzles, calculators and printable planners. Its word unscrambler turns a jumble of letters into every word they can make, which is handy for Scrabble, Words With Friends, Wordle, crosswords and anagram games. Alongside it you will find word games and puzzles to play, and 70+ easy calculators for money, maths, health and everyday sums.

How to use it

Type your letters, then press GO. Use ? or * for a blank tile. Tap any word to look up its meaning. Use the options to narrow results by length, start, end or letters inside.

Scores and word list

Each word shows its tile score, with the highest scoring words first. Every tile shows its Scrabble letter value, and a blue number appears above it when Words With Friends scores that letter differently. Each word shows its Scrabble total in grey and, when different, its Words With Friends total in blue. Words come from our US or UK Scrabble list, explained under Our dictionary below.

Looking for more? Open the menu for the anagram solver, word games and puzzles, the calculators and the free printable planners. Read more about gounscramble.

If a letter in Starts with, Ends with or Contains is missing from your tiles, it is added. A wildcard tile scores 0.

    How to use the word unscrambler

    1. Type your letters into the box. You can enter up to 15, and use ? or * for a blank tile.
    2. Press Unscramble. Words are grouped by length, with the longest first.
    3. Open More options to pick a dictionary or to narrow the list with Starts with, Ends with, Contains and Word length.
    4. No letters? Leave the letters box empty and use only those options to list every word that starts with, ends with or contains something, or has a certain length.

    Each letter can be used only as many times as you typed it, so TEAR can make rate but not tree, which needs two Es. Words do not have to use every letter. To find only words that use all of them, use the anagram solver.

    Good for

    Our dictionary

    The US Scrabble list in gounscramble is ENABLE, the Enhanced North American Benchmark Lexicon. It is a free, public-domain word list of about 173,000 English words, widely used for word games and puzzles. Because it is public domain, we can use it freely and show you every word it contains.

    The UK Scrabble list is larger, with about 245,000 words, including more British spellings. Use the US / UK switch under the search box to choose. Words With Friends uses its own unpublished list, so US Scrabble is the closest match. Neither list is official, so check disputed words against your own game. Offensive words are hidden by default; use the toggle to show them. Learn more in word lists explained and credits.

    Common questions

    How does the word unscrambler work?

    You type the letters you have. The tool checks every word in its dictionary and keeps the ones you can spell using those letters, with each letter used at most as many times as you typed it. Results are grouped by length, longest first.

    What is the difference between the unscrambler and the anagram solver?

    The unscrambler shows words of every length that can be made from some or all of your letters. The anagram solver shows only the words that use every one of your letters.

    How do I use a blank tile?

    Type ? or * in place of the unknown letter. A blank can stand for any letter, but it counts as a tile, so it is used up when it fills a place.

    How many letters can I enter?

    Up to 15 letters, including blanks.

    Can I search without typing any letters?

    Yes. Leave the letters box empty, open More options, and fill in Starts with, Ends with, Contains or Word length. The tool then lists every word that matches, for example all five-letter words that start with a and end with e.
