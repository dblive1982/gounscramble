Drag a piece onto the grid. A full row or column clears. If a clearing line spells a word of 3 to 5 letters, forwards or backwards, it turns gold and scores a bonus. Clear lines on back-to-back moves to build a streak multiplier.

How to play Word Block

Word Block is a free block puzzle with a word twist. Every piece carries letters. Fill a whole row or column to clear it, and if the cleared line contains a word, you score extra. Pieces with a straight run of letters often spell a word, so line them up before you clear.

The game ends when none of your three pieces fit. Use a hint to see a move that clears a line. Play the Daily puzzle to get the same pieces as everyone else today.
